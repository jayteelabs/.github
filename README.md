# JayTee Labs

**JayTee Labs builds practical AI systems for real-world work.**

We focus on agentic software, developer tooling, automation, and human-in-the-loop workflows — the kind of infrastructure that helps people move faster without handing the steering wheel to a black box.

Our work sits at the intersection of:

- **AI agents** — systems that can reason, execute, coordinate, and recover
- **Developer tools** — workflows that make engineering sharper, not noisier
- **Automation** — boring things handled reliably so humans can focus
- **Product systems** — turning messy ideas into usable, maintainable software

We care about software that is useful, inspectable, and durable. Fancy demos are nice. Systems that keep working after the demo are better.

## What we build

JayTee Labs projects usually fall into a few buckets:

- Autonomous and semi-autonomous agent workflows
- AI-assisted engineering systems
- Knowledge, memory, and research infrastructure
- Internal tooling for operations and product development
- Experiments that may or may not become something bigger

## Principles

- **Human judgment stays in the loop where it matters**
- **Good tooling should reduce cognitive load, not create more of it**
- **Clear systems beat clever chaos**
- **Shipping matters**
- **Documentation is part of the product**

## Status

Some repositories are polished. Some are experiments. Some are weird little prototypes with sharp edges and a suspicious amount of potential.

That’s the lab part.
